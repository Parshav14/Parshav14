## About Me

I'm a Computer Science engineer who ships things — not just models in a notebook, but systems that run in production. My work splits across three lanes: **applied ML/NLP research** (first-author, Elsevier Q1 *Knowledge-Based Systems*), **speech AI** (Samsung PRISM, PESQ 4.64 vocoder work, Springer-published), and **backend/enterprise engineering** (currently a Software Developer at Flick Network, building e-invoicing integrations across UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Oman).

I like the parts of AI work most people skip past: cleaning the pipeline until the metrics are trustworthy, reading the paper twice before implementing it, and making sure the thing actually deploys instead of just training.

- 🔭 Currently deep in **LLM agents, RAG pipelines, and agentic frameworks**
- 🌱 Working through agentic AI engineering coursework alongside my day job
- 🤝 Getting involved in open-source ML contributions ahead of GSoC 2027
- ⚡ Fun fact: my speech-synthesis research runs at PESQ 4.64 — better sound quality than most people notice they're listening to
